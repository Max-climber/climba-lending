Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Climba collects, uses, and protects user information when using the Climba mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Climba may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: email address, user ID, and authentication data used to sign in to the app.
  • App activity data: route views, logged climbs, attempts, ratings, feedback, league participation, and other actions performed inside the app.
  • Technical data: device information, app version, crash reports, diagnostics, and performance data.
  • Optional user content: information that users voluntarily provide, such as route feedback or ratings.

We do not collect sensitive personal data unless it is necessary for the operation of the service and explicitly provided by the user.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • provide access to the Climba app;
  • allow users to log climbs and track progress;
  • display routes, leagues, rankings, and activity history;
  • improve app functionality and user experience;
  • analyze app performance and fix technical issues;
  • provide climbing gyms with aggregated and anonymized analytics;
  • ensure security and prevent misuse of the service.

3. Data Sharing

Climba does not sell personal data.

We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate the app, including hosting, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, and infrastructure providers.

Climbing gyms may receive aggregated or anonymized information about route activity, ratings, and user engagement. Personal user information is not shared with climbing gyms unless required for a specific app function or with the user's consent.

4. Authentication

Users can sign in using email verification, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple. Authentication providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We keep user data only for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve the product.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting us.

6. User Rights

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data by contacting Climba.

7. Children's Privacy

Climba is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate consent where required by law.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
